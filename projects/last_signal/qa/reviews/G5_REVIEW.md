# G5 review (re-review after drift) — Storyboard and shots

**Verdict: FAIL (advisory).** Most of the post-approval changes fix real
problems the first preview found, and the rationales are good. Three SC03
shots should not be re-approved as they stand:
- SC03_SH030 breaks a locked tilt limit.
- SC03_SH060's own numbers put the lens 0.11 m from the phone, and its frames
  come out blurred.
- SC03_SH020 never shows the bolt that is its whole beat.

SC01 and SC03_SH050-070 cannot be judged on pictures yet, because their
previews are stale.

**Recommendation for `fm approve G5`:** wait. Route findings 1-3 to their
owners first. Decide CHANGE-002 and CHANGE-003, which the revised SC01 and SC03
geometry depends on. Re-approve after the laptop re-render of SC01,
SC03_SH020/SH030/SH060 and SC03_SH050-070, with a fresh playblast sheet. If you
want to re-approve now anyway, the SC04 revisions and the storyboard/shot-list
text are the safe part. The SC03 items are not.

Scope: the review covers the G5 approval at commit `8e9b8b7` (2026-09-29 15:29)
through HEAD `0ac12ba` (2026-10-02 13:13). I diffed every drifted node
(`git diff 8e9b8b7 HEAD`, hash and stamp lines excluded).

## What the human should look at first
1. **SC03 is the weak scene after the re-blocking.** The kneel spot moved to
   (3.45, 8.66) so his hand can reach the socket, and every SC03 lens moved
   with it. As a result:
   - SC03_SH030 now tilts up about 20°, against the locked 8° maximum in
     `camera.height.low` (finding 1).
   - SC03_SH060's lens is about 0.11 m from the phone. The frame is about
     26 mm instead of 71 mm, and the "2 %" renders as a blur (finding 2).
   - SC03_SH020 never shows the phone's face, so the bolt appearing, the
     scene's moment of hope, does not read (finding 3).
2. **SC01_SH090 no longer shows the thumb.** The approved screenplay says "He
   freezes, thumb in the air" (SCREENPLAY line 93). The revision deliberately
   frames only the deadpan face. It is a reasoned choice, but it changes an
   approved story image, so it is your call (finding 4).
3. **SC04_SH030, the first push of the turn, never shows his eyes.** The spec
   wants a three-quarter face for f0-9. The current still and the review
   frames (f0, f3, f10, f16, f28, f35) show the top of his head looking down,
   then the back of his head. The decision beat lands on hair (finding 5).
