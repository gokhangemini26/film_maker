# G5 review — Storyboard and shots

**Verdict: WARN.** The camera plan serves all six locked intents. It breaks no
locked canon, and `fm check continuity` is clean. The gag, the turn, the send
and the ending are staged with care. Nothing QA found makes a shot impossible
to produce. Before camera and continuity canon lock, though, a few things need
your attention. One rule about to lock is worded backwards. The final image and
the crank choice are short of the "quiet and slow" they are meant to have.
Hana's window, the film's only sky link, barely reaches her close-up. And
several PROPOSED entries carry stale text that would lock with them.

## What the human should look at first
1. **The phone-lit rule is worded so that it fails the shots it is meant to
   protect, and it locks at this gate** (finding 1). `camera.phone_lit.framing`
   puts the lens "within 45 degrees of the direction the screen faces". A
   screen faces its owner, so that describes a camera looking over his
   shoulder, not one looking at his lit face. By QA's geometry every SC04 face
   shot is about 140° from the screen's facing direction. It is about 38° from
   the line between his face and the screen, which is clearly the intended
   meaning. The new SC05 exemption ("up to 75 degrees") is also not met by the
   SC05 staging, which QA computes at about 91–116°. This is cheap to reword
   now and needs a change request once it is locked.
2. **The two most tender beats are the most compressed** (findings 2 and 3).
   The final image (SC06_SH010) reaches its pose around f56 and the fade-out
   starts at f64. That leaves about 8 frames at full brightness, even though 10
   frames were moved into SC06 at G2 so the ending could breathe. The choice of
   the crank (SC04_SH040) fits seven actions into 60 frames. Its own animation
   notes say it needs about 12 more frames to read as slow. And as the lean-in
   is specified, his hand does not reach the glovebox.
3. **Hana's scene** (finding 4, Q1). The three-shot plan is the right answer to
   "his photo must be recognisable" with the locked desk. But in the close-up
   where she smiles (SC05_SH030), the window takes only about 11 % of the frame
   width and is seen almost edge-on (*computed*). In practice the "same sky"
   link rests on the 1.33 s medium. Decide the three-shot question knowing
   this.
