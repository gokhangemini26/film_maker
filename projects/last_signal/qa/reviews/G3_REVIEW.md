# G3 review — World and characters

**Verdict: WARN.** The world and the two characters follow every locked
intent, tone and story entry, and both designers have worked from each other's
numbers. Nothing here contradicts canon or cannot be built. You should still
look at a few things before approving, because G3 approval locks these entries
and later changes will need change requests. One is a real staging error in
Hana's room. One is the G2 door gap, which a single line in the Art Direction
Bible now keeps open. The rest are scale checks and choices only you can make.

## What the human should look at first
1. **Hana's room can't give the angle both designers describe (finding 1).**
   - Her desk is *against the window wall, under the window*
     (`world.sets.hana_room` `desk.against: window_wall`), so she faces the window.
   - `characters.hana.proportions` and `characters.hana.silhouette` both rest
     on "the dusk sky … right behind her head in the main angle" and "framed
     against the west window, partly backlit".
   - The ART_DIRECTION_BIBLE §Hana's room says the main angle looks "from
     inside the room toward the window", with her face first in the
     hierarchy. From there the camera sees the back of her head.
   - With the room as specified, you can see her face or the window behind her
     head, not both. SC05 carries the smile, the film's only answer, and the
     only visual link between the two characters is that shared sky. This is
     cheap to fix now. It will be expensive at G5.
2. **Door continuity (G2 finding 3) is still open. It can be fixed without
   reopening G2 (finding 2).**
   - The approved SCREENPLAY SC02 says only "The car door swings open"; it
     does not say which door.
   - The ART_DIRECTION_BIBLE §Street "Entrances and eyelines" commits him to
     "the car's driver door (road side)".
   - WORLD_BIBLE storytelling detail 4 and the SC04 hierarchy then rely on the
     *passenger* door standing open, and nothing opens it.
   - If he leaves by the kerb-side passenger door (the shop's side) and leaves
     it open, both approved texts hold and the gap closes. See Q5.
3. **Approving G3 locks the colour hexes before the look phase exists (Q4).**
   Every `characters.*.colors` and wardrobe `color` is a hex proposal. The
   character-designer says the look-director may change them "via change
   request once approved". Decide whether that is what you want (QA thinks it
   is).
