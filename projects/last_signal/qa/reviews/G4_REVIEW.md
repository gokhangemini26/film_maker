# G4 review — Visual direction (style lock)

**Verdict: WARN.** The look serves all six locked intents and breaks no locked
canon. It closes G3 finding 8 (red rule, amber pip and ring) and responds to G3
finding 9 (originality). Most of its computed figures check out when re-run. It
should still not be locked as it stands without your attention. Four decisions
are yours (the red heart, Hana's room, how restrained the sky is, and the jacket
at dusk). The tender section departs from the approved direction's colour arc
without saying so. A few style-lock numbers contradict each other, and those
would produce false FAILs at G6.

## What the human should look at first
1. **The red heart (Q1, findings 2–3).** `look.color.ui_heart` asks you to allow
   one exception to "battery red is the only red". The heart is on screen next to
   the red 1–2 % battery. The rule's own rationale bans the red crank pip for
   exactly that reason: two reds side by side muddy the stake (G3 finding 8). The
   look-director's rationale never considers a third option that keeps the rule
   absolute, a pastel rose heart with an ink outline. Separately, nothing
   specifies the emoji panel he must open to type the heart. A realistic panel
   would put dozens of coloured emoji, red and pink among them, on screen.
2. **The ending's light (findings 1 and 5).** The approved CREATIVE_DIRECTION asks
   that "the crank and send move toward warmer, dimmer pastels". The look makes
   them cooler, with warmth only in his hands (`look.color.scene.sc04` dominant
   `#6F7099`, a cool lavender). That is a defensible idea, but it is undeclared.
   Also, with hard two-tone cel shading, the 24-frame fade of his phone in SC06
   would not appear on his face as a fade. His face would snap from lit to shadow
   in one frame, which is the "cut, not fade" the lighting rationale rejects.
3. **The jacket against the dusk sky (Q4, finding 8).** Lit by his phone, the
   jacket measures **1.01:1** against the dusk zenith (the bible says "about
   1.2:1"). The approved direction asks for "low angles when he makes up his
   mind", which means the turn in SC04 at dusk, and that is exactly the angle
   the COLOR_BIBLE tells the cinematographer to avoid. The bible's fallback
   colour (`#7FA6C0`) only gets to 1.11:1.
