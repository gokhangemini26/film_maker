# Preview review — 38 pinned stills, v6 (commit 551eead)

**Verdict: FAIL** (advisory). Counts: **PASS 5 · WARN 15 · FAIL 18** (previous review: PASS 1 · WARN 10 · FAIL 27).
G6 is already approved as a block-out. This verdict does not reopen it. It says which stills are fit to judge
framing and story beats now, and what still blocks G6-quality previews.

## Delta since the previous review
v6 fixed the largest problem class. The phone now draws real UI: map and pin, call screen with photo, compose
field, status bar with coloured digits, battery icon and amber bolt, and a sent bubble with an amber progress line
and a tick. The amber placeholder bar is gone. The climax insert SC04_SH080 now reads as "message sent". Inserts
are upright and square to the lens, and a ray-cast sight-line clearance was added. Phones are held in SC04 and by
Hana (SC05_SH030). A crank prop is built wherever a shot lists it, in canon crank_body #D6C592, and its pip is
the only amber (lit in SC04_SH090). The car is canon butter cream. Sampled mean frame colour is cool-biased in
5/38 stills (was 26/38), and 0/15 SC01 golden-hour stills are cool (was 12/15). SC03_SH030 and SC03_SH070 went
from FAIL to WARN (face readable; eyes no longer emissive). Still unfixed: SC01 passenger-side occlusion
(SH060/100/130), the roof slab in SH150, SC02_SH010 and SC03_SH020 poses, the missing mouth, and Hana's
silhouette. v6 also brought **new defects**. The battery state table in `phone.py:30-43` is wrong for three
shots and contradicts locked `continuity.battery`. The tick is drawn upside down. The heart is never drawn.
Both phone photos are the same avatar. The new SC04 "eyes-on-crank" aim (`preview.py:226-227`) now cuts
Ren's head out of SC04_SH050 and SH090.

## What the human should look at first
1. **The battery story is told wrong in three inserts (locked canon contradicted).** SC01_SH080 shows the amber
   bolt beside 4 % before the engine has even started. SC01_SH120 shows **3 %** + bolt where canon has 4 % + bolt,
   so the drop to 3 % in SH140 becomes invisible. SC04_SH020 drops the "1 %" digits (canon: icon blinks, digits
   stay). Cause: `phone.py:30-43` (`n >= 70` → 4 %+bolt; `n >= 120` → 3 %+bolt; SC04 `digits=False`). This is
   the spine of `intent.race_against_battery`.
2. **Hana's payoff does not read.** SC05_SH010: the screen is out of focus. His photo uses the same avatar design as
   *her* photo in SC01_SH030/050 (one generic avatar, `phone.py:119-123`), so "it's him" cannot read. SC05_SH030:
   the smile is the shot, but there is no mouth, and Hana has Ren's bowl haircut with no bob and no headphones.
3. **SC04's hope sequence is faceless or crankless.** SH040 has no crank in frame. SH050 and SH090 frame torso
   to knees and cut off the head, so the release beat (breath out, shoulders drop) is lost. SH060 has no heart, so the
   tender beat is identical to SC01_SH070.
