# Lighting Bible: Last Signal

PROPOSED for G4. Canon: `look.lighting.*` (strategy and per-scene), light colours
in `look.color.light_sources`, exposure in `look.exposure.*`. Coordinates use the
shared street frame from `world.sets.street`: +Y west (toward the sun and the shop),
+X north (across the road), Z up, origin on the south kerb at the car's passenger door.

## Principles (`look.lighting.principles`)
1. **Every light has a world source (DECISION).** Each light in a shot names a source
   from the motivation maps below. There is no unnamed fill or rim. A fill is "bounce
   from X"; a rim is "the afterglow" or "the fridges". (Style lock L10.)
2. **The key moves from the world to him (DECISION).** In the comedy the key is the
   world's light: the sun, then the shop's ceiling panels. From the blackout on, the key
   is his own light: the phone, then the crank pip in his hand. That is `story.theme`
   (borrowed power fails, his own power works) told in light, and it serves
   `intent.earned_last_signal`.
3. **At the end, warmth passes to the sky (DECISION).** When his phone goes dark the
   afterglow, the sky Hana is under, finds his upturned face. The last warm light is
   not from a device (`intent.open_hopeful_ending`).
4. **Light failing is a story event (from CREATIVE_DIRECTION).** The car's lights go out
   in the stall frame; the shop's go out in one frame on the clunk. Street lamps and
   windows never change on screen, and the shop never comes back
   (`world.rules.power`, `tone.the_turn`).
5. **Soft but cinematic.** Hard keys in the comic golden hour give graphic cel shapes.
   Soft keys in the tender dusk. Shadows are always tinted, never black, except in the
   SC03 blackout.

### Scene notes
- **SC01 (`look.lighting.sc01`).** The sun is 6° up along +Y and fills the windscreen.
  Keep the backlit silhouette (tuft, jacket panels) as designed
  (`characters.ren.silhouette`). No interior fill light beyond the dashboard bounce.
  The stall: engine stops → in the same frame the dash lights and the amber ring go
  out, the bolt vanishes, and the phone dips once for 3 frames. There is no other
  light change, and no smoke (`world.rules.power`).
- **SC02 (`look.lighting.sc02`).** The most open exterior. Long shadows fall east
  toward the car, the most graphic golden-hour cue a cel look has. Street lamps are off
  (daylight).
- **SC03 (`look.lighting.sc03`).** Lit: bright, uniform, near-shadowless; the fridges
  glow in front of him and edge his silhouette as he kneels at the back-right corner. **Blackout event:** on the clunk, in
  one frame, panels, fridges and fascia go to zero. Nothing fades. The phone becomes the
  only key for the first time (the glow motif is born). The door rectangle stays as a
  faint warm shape far behind. The unlit side of his face drops to `#1E2030`. No fill,
  no emergency light.
- **SC04 (`look.lighting.sc04`).** His kerb is in the unlit gap between lamps
  (`world.sets.street`), so the phone wins. The 1 % flicker (irregular dips to 70 %,
  2-3 frames each) runs until the **first crank turn**. From then the glow holds steady,
  as the screenplay says ("The glow on his face stops flickering and holds"). The pip
  glows amber only while the handle turns and dies within 6 frames of it stopping. The
  progress line and tick are amber. He lets go: the pip fades out, the phone stays
  steady.
- **SC05 (`look.lighting.sc05`).** The softest, most balanced frame: warm lamp on one
  side, cool sky ahead, then the phone's warm white arrives from the other side as a
  new light on her face as the smile lands. That also supports the CHARACTER_BIBLE's
  smile fallback 2 ("let the phone's light arrive on her face as the smile lands").
- **SC06 (`look.lighting.sc06`).** The screen fades from full to off over 24 frames
  (ease-out): gentle, not a punchline (`tone.ending_restraint`). Nothing else changes: no
  lamp switches, the shop stays dark. With the phone gone, the afterglow from the west
  catches his upturned face and the tuft. The final image is lower in key but lifted
  and soft, with one warm edge.

## Arc of light across the film
1. **The world lights him (SC01-SC02).** Low golden sun, hard and warm, from the west.
   He is often a backlit silhouette: the comedy watches his shape.
2. **The world's light in a box (SC03, lit).** Cool, bright, shadowless shop light:
   order, and a place he runs into for help.
3. **The world's light fails (SC03, blackout).** One frame to black. The phone is the
   only light, from below. His own light is born, still borrowed from the battery.
4. **Twilight, and his own light (SC04).** The sun has gone off screen
   (`world.rules.time_and_sun`). The ambient turns cool, and the key is the phone in his
   hands, flickering until he cranks, then steady, with the small amber pip he makes
   himself.
5. **The same light arrives elsewhere (SC05).** The phone's warm white lands on Hana's
   face as a new light, beside her own warm lamp and under the same sky.
6. **Warmth passes to the sky (SC06).** His screen fades. The afterglow of the sky she is
   under finds his face as he looks up.

The mean frame luminance follows the same line (`look.exposure.scene_keys`):
0.62 → 0.70 → 0.74 → 0.08 → 0.46 → 0.52 → 0.42.

## Colour temperature split
- **Before the turn:** warm key (sun ~3200K, `#FFD9A0`) against cool fill (sky
  ~9000K, `#C8D3EA`); the shop is the one cool-keyed space (~5000-6500K).
- **After the turn:** cool ambient (dusk sky ~10000K, `#9CA2D0`) against warm, small,
  close sources: phone `#FFF1DE`, pip `#F5B940`, afterglow `#F6C69C`, and in the
  background lamps and windows (~2700K, saturation 0.40 or below).
- The phone's colour is a stylised warm white rather than a real screen's cool 6500K,
  so the screen reads as company, not as cold technology (`tone.anti_goals`:
  anti-tech message; `look.color.phone_glow`).
- Hex governs the render; the Kelvin figures record the physical motivation.

## Shadows
- **Golden hour:** hard, long, falling east (−Y). The A-pillar and roof shadow bars
  across Ren in the car are the main graphic shapes of SC01.
- **Shop, lit:** almost none (top-lit, soft): uniform order.
- **Blackout:** the phone's under-light puts shadows upward (brows, nose, fringe). His
  worry reads in the lifted brows (`characters.ren.face`).
- **Dusk:** soft and short, from the sky above; the phone's shadows are small and soft.
  Nothing hides his face after the turn.
- Shadow colour always follows the scene shadow tint (COLOR_BIBLE); never neutral.

## Feasibility notes for EEVEE (`look.lighting.eevee_feasibility`)
- **Budget:** at most 8 lights per shot. Typical counts: SC01 4 (sun, bounce, dash
  group, ring); SC02 2; SC03 lit 6 (panel group, fridge group, door, phone),
  blackout 2; SC04 5 (sky world, afterglow area, phone, pip, shop lamp); SC05 3;
  SC06 3.
- **Sky:** world colour gradient plus a sun lamp (golden hour); no HDRI (licence
  UNKNOWN, photographic).
- **Afterglow rim:** one large, low area light far down +Y (beyond the railing at
  y = 30), about 20 m wide, warm `#F6C69C`. It is motivated by the western sky, so it
  is not an unmotivated rim.
- **Phone cast light:** an emissive screen plane (what the camera sees) plus one small
  area light the size of the screen (0.07 × 0.15 m), `#FFF1DE`, with its power animated
  for the dips, flicker and fade.
- **Crank pip:** a tiny point light with short range (~0.1 m custom distance) plus the
  emissive pip.
- **Blackout:** keyframe every shop light and emissive to zero on the same frame.
  ASSUMPTION to verify at M3: EEVEE's world lighting may leak into interiors. For SC03
  (and the car interior) use a shop-specific world or view layer with world strength
  near zero after the blackout, so the only light left is the phone and the door
  rectangle.
- **Toon response:** cel steps come from the Shader to RGB ramp, so a small light (pip,
  phone) makes a small hard-edged lit patch, which is what the look wants. Check at G6
  that the phone's lit patch on his face is large enough to read (adjust light radius,
  not add fill).
- **No volumetrics, no ray tracing, default light probes.** No god rays at golden hour;
  depth from the aerial tint (`look.style.atmosphere`).
- **ASSUMPTION:** Blender 5.2 EEVEE behaves like 4.x for all of the above (area/point/
  sun lights, custom distance, Shader to RGB, world per view layer or scene). Verify
  with the M3 builder.

## Dependencies and open items
- **G3 finding 1 (Hana's room layout)** is still open in canon. SC05 lighting is
  written relative to her and works for either fix. The shared-sky rule (L13) needs the
  window in her frame.
- **Kerb sitting spot:** the plan assumes the ART_DIRECTION_BIBLE recommendation (rear
  quarter, y ≈ −1.0, facing the pavement), which puts the afterglow on his right. If
  the cinematographer moves him, the rim side moves with the west, not with the camera.
- Light intensities (watts) are not set here. They are calibrated at G6 against
  `look.exposure.scene_keys` and L9 (phone brightest near his face).
