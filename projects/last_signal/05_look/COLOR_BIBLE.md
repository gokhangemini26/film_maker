# Colour Bible: Last Signal

PROPOSED for G4. Every hex below is in `canon/look.yaml` (`look.color.*`) as
`#RRGGBB`; the M3 resolver converts to linear RGB. Numbers marked *computed* were
measured with a script from the hex values (HSV, WCAG relative luminance, and the
lit/shadow formula in `look.style.character_shading`). They describe the palette,
not a render: renders are checked at G6.

## Colour intent
The film is soft and light so that two small things can carry the story by colour
alone. **Red is the stake**: it lives only on the battery (and, if the human agrees,
on the heart he deletes). **Amber is power**: one colour on every sign of power
flowing, which fails twice and works under his own hand. Around them the world is a
warm cream in light and a lavender in shadow. At the turn the whole film slides from
the warm side to the cool side. After the turn, warmth survives only in what he holds,
and at the very end in the sky's afterglow on his face.

**The progression.** Warm and bright (SC01-SC02) → the brightest cool space (SC03) →
one-frame drop to the film's only black → cool, lifted dusk where warmth is only in
his hands (SC04) → the one balanced warm/cool frame (SC05) → cool dusk with a warm
edge from the sky (SC06). This follows the direction's energy curve: two brisk peaks,
then a long exhale. Target frame means: `look.exposure.scene_keys`.

**The one sky.** SC04, SC05 and SC06 use one dusk sky asset, unchanged
(`look.color.sky.dusk`). The distant silhouettes behind his street and in her window
share `#5F6088`. The sky does not darken between SC04 and SC06, because SC05 is the
same minute as the send (`world.rules.time_and_sun`) and a darkening sky would make
the ending feel like nightfall. The final image gets lower in key because the phone
goes out, not because the sky changes.

## Relationships and saturation rules

**Warm/cool split.** Warm cream (`#F1E4CF`) in light against lavender (`#A79FC6`) in
shadow throughout. Before the turn the key light is warm and the shadows cool. After
the turn the ambient is cool and warmth comes only from sources near him (phone,
crank body, pip, afterglow rim).

**Saturation strategy (`look.color.rule.pastel_range`).** World surfaces, props, sky
and lights stay at HSV saturation 0.45 or below with lit value 0.35 or above.
*Computed:* every world, prop, sky and light colour in canon passes. The only colours
above 0.45 are the three accents, the map pin (UI, `#4A6FA5`, S 0.55, governed by
`look.color.ui`) and the key darks. Sky saturation stays at 0.40 or below
(`look.style.originality_guards`).

**Battery red is the only red (`look.color.rule.only_red`, DECISION).** No colour in
the red hue band 345-20° above saturation 0.35, and none in the pink band 300-345°
above 0.35, anywhere in the film except the battery (and the heart exception).
*Computed:* 189 colours checked (all look and character canon hexes), 0 violations.
Near-misses that pass: dusk peach `#EFC3AE` (hue 19°, S 0.27), dusty mauve `#D7BFC8`
(hue 338°, S 0.11), Ren's skin in the SC01 shadow `#A88487` (hue 355°, S 0.21).
This resolves **G3 finding 8**:
- **Crank indicator pip:** power amber `#F5B940`, not red. It glows only while he turns.
- **Car adapter ring light:** power amber `#F5B940`, not red. It goes out with the dash.
- Awning stripes are mint/cream, posters and merchandise are pastel families with no
  red, and the map pin is blue.

**Red vs amber.** *Computed:* hue gap 36° (red about 5°, amber about 40°), at similar
saturation (0.74). Amber is much lighter (relative luminance 0.55 vs 0.22), so the two
separate by value as well as hue when the bolt sits beside the red number (SC01, SC03).
Warm practicals (street lamp `#FFD6A0`, windows `#F6DCA6`, desk lamp `#FFD29A`, sun
`#FFD9A0`) sit at saturation 0.33-0.40, well below amber's 0.74, so they read as warm
light, not as the accent (L7).

**Accent budget.** Three saturated colours, each with one job, and each introduced in
SC01: green (call, once), red (stake), amber (power). SC05 has no accent at all.

### The heart (human question)
`look.color.ui_heart` proposes that the heart he types and deletes in SC04 is battery
red. It is the one exception to the red rule, on screen for about 2 s, inside the phone.
- **For:** a red heart reads as "love" instantly, with no words. Sharing the battery's
  red ties the thing he is risking to the stake, and deleting it takes the second red
  off the screen, which is what he keeps for the dinner.
- **Against:** it weakens "the only red" from absolute to "only red except once". An
  ink-dark heart (`#2B303B`) keeps the rule absolute, but reads sadder and less
  immediately.
- RECOMMENDATION: keep the red heart. If the human prefers the absolute rule, change
  `look.color.ui_heart` to `#2B303B` and remove the exception from
  `look.color.rule.only_red` before approval.
