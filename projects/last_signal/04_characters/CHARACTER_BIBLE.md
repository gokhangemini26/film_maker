# Character Bible: Last Signal

PROPOSED for G3. Canon lives in `canon/characters.yaml`. This bible explains it
and adds the continuity tables. Sizes are in metres. Every hex colour is a
proposal to coordinate with the look-director, who owns the palette (LOOK phase).

**Ground rules for both characters**
- *USER_REQUIREMENT (via `story.cast`):* two characters, both about 20, both
  adults. Designed as 20: adult proportions and adult clothing, never childlike,
  never sexualised (`tone.anti_goals`).
- *USER_REQUIREMENT (via `tone.wordless`), implemented as DECISION:* **no readable
  text on any clothing or accessory.** No print, logo, label, badge or stripe
  pattern that could read as lettering. Names are production ids only and
  never appear on screen.
- *DECISION:* **no red or pink anywhere on either character**, including blush.
  The battery's red is the film's stake, and it should be the only red the eye
  finds.
- *DECISION:* **no watches, clocks or bags.** A watch is a second meter
  (`story.escalation`), and a bag invites "why not a power bank?"
  (`world.rules.power`).
- *FACT (project rule, ORIGINALITY):* both designs are original. They are built
  from generic, everyday garments and one signature shape each (his tuft, her
  headphones on a bob), not from any existing character, costume or real
  person. QA should still check the finished proxies against well-known anime
  protagonists, because a common combination (blue jacket, tuft of hair) could
  drift toward one in the look pass.

## Ren

### Identity (`characters.ren.identity`)
Ren is 20, a university student, and the protagonist. He has finally found the
nerve to invite Hana to dinner, where he means to tell her he loves her.
- **Want:** get the invitation to her before his phone dies.
- **Need:** stop reaching for shortcuts and put his own effort in (`story.arc.ren`).
- **Defining behaviour (DECISION):** *the earnest reset.* After every setback he
  freezes, sags, then straightens and tries again. This is the engine of the
  comedy (`tone.comedy_source`: bad timing plus persistence) and what the turn
  breaks. At the kerb he does not bounce back up; he settles.
- He is never clumsy. Every failure is the world's timing, not his fault, so we
  laugh with him and never at him (`tone.register`).
- Carries: phone (in hand), charging cable, car key (stays in the ignition),
  crank charger (glovebox, then his hands). Nothing else.

### Silhouette (`characters.ren.silhouette`)
Silhouette comes first: in the backlit car, the raking street and the dark shop
his face is small or shadowed, so three shapes carry him.
1. **The crown tuft.** One stubborn tuft standing up at the crown, 0.04 m
   above the hair line. It reads on the head outline even against the sun in
   the windscreen, and it is already in the script: the mirror beat in SC01.
2. **The open jacket with a pale centre strip.** A hip-length jacket always
   worn open over an oat T-shirt: two blue panels and a pale vertical strip. In
   the dark shop, lit only by the phone, the strip is what reads as "him".
3. **Posture as arc.** Before the turn he is a forward-leaning "?" (neck jutting,
   shoulders curled over the phone). After the turn he is upright and settled.
   In the final image he is a relaxed diagonal leaning back.

*RECOMMENDATION for the cinematographer:* below about 15 % of frame height, only
posture reads, not the tuft or the strip. Keep him above that in any shot where
his state matters.

*Rejected:* a hood or a cap as his signature. It hides the tuft and the mirror
beat, and it reads as "hiding" rather than "hopeful".

**Stylisation (DECISION):** head, eyes and hands are about 10 % larger than
realistic, at 6.5 heads. That gives the expressive faces and readable hands the
direction asks for while still reading as a believable 20-year-old.
Super-deformed proportions would tip the comedy into manic slapstick
(`tone.anti_goals`).

**Headroom (DEPENDENCY, for the M3 build and the cinematographer):** with the
driver's seat upright, his crown plus tuft reaches about 1.41 m. The car's roof
is 1.45 m on the outside, and the interior headliner is not specified in world
canon. A seat back reclined about 20° (normal for a driver) brings it to about
1.36 m. If the interior is lower, the tuft will clip the headliner. This should
be checked in the first proxy test (G6), not solved with a gag.

**Kerb seating (RECOMMENDATION, confirms the world's spot):** he sits on the
pavement edge facing the pavement, knees up, with his back leaning on the car's
rear side panel. His seated depth (~0.6 m) fits on the 1.8 m pavement, and the
lean closes the 0.2 m gap between the kerb and the car body. The open passenger
door stays forward of him, so his eyeline to the glovebox runs through it.

### Face and hair (`characters.ren.face`)
Only what the film shows is designed.
- **Face:** slightly long, soft oval; soft jaw. Longer than Hana's round face.
- **Brows:** straight and level, able to knit and lift. His worry lives here,
  because brows are the strongest readable feature under the single low lights
  he is seen in (backlit in the car, phone-lit in the dark shop and at the kerb).
- **Eyes:** medium almond. **Nose:** small, simple. **Mouth:** a line that can
  flatten, press or open slightly.
- **Hair:** short, soft, a little messy, dark brown `#4A3A31`, fringe down to the
  brows (framing the eyes, never covering them), plus the crown tuft.
- **Colours (proposed):** skin `#EAC7AB`, eyes `#5B4637`, brows `#3E3029`.
- **None of:** glasses (screen reflections would fight the phone glow),
  freckles, scars, piercings.
- Not tied to any nationality, consistent with `world.setting` (no country
  markers).

### Expressions (`characters.ren.expressions`)
Two sets, split at the turn (`tone.the_turn`). After the crank begins, he never
uses a comic expression again.

| Comic set (SC01–SC03) | Where |
|---|---|
| Rehearsed breath: cheeks fill, eyes shut, then open | SC01 before the call |
| Letdown: inner brows up, mouth flat | SC01 call unanswered |
| Freeze: eyes wide, pupils small, whole body held | SC01 battery turns red |
| Relief: eyes close, long exhale, head back | SC01 bolt appears; SC03 bolt appears |
| Face hidden: forehead on the wheel | SC01 car dies |
| Stunned stillness: face lit from below, brows lifted, no movement | SC03 blackout |

| Tender set (SC04, SC06) | Where |
|---|---|
| Focused calm: brows level, eyes on the phone | SC04 cranking |
| Hesitation: lips pressed, a slow blink | SC04 heart typed, then deleted |
| Release: eyes close, breath out | SC04 after the tick |
| Final look up: lids low, mouth soft, the faintest ease at the corners | SC06 final image |

- Comic reactions are **held poses**, not rubbery takes. The comedy is in the
  timing.
- **No graphic comic symbols** (sweat drops, anger marks, sparkles) and **no
  super-deformed faces** (DECISION). They are the loud end of anime comedy and
  would break "framed and lit like cinema" (`intent.soft_but_cinematic`). *See
  open question 1.*
- No big grin at the tick. The triumph is small and earned (`intent.earned_last_signal`).
- No lip-sync, and the mouth never opens more than slightly (`tone.wordless`).

| Scene | Wardrobe state | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| SC01 car | Full outfit, jacket open, bunched at the back against the seat | Sleeves up, so both forearms are clear for the phone, the mirror and the glovebox |
| SC02 street | Same | Jacket fronts swing with the run (keyframed or a simple secondary motion, no cloth sim) |
| SC03 shop | Same; kneeling | Knees stay clean afterwards: no dirt marks (that would be a humiliation gag, excluded by `tone.comedy_source`) |
| SC04 kerb | Same; sitting knees-up | Jacket falls open around him on the pavement; oat strip faces the phone light |
| SC06 kerb | Same; leaning back | Dark phone rests on the oat strip on his chest, under one flat hand |

**Why this jacket (DECISION, `characters.ren.wardrobe.jacket`):** it is his biggest
colour block and the main carrier of his silhouette. A cool, medium-value blue
separates him from the warm golden hour and from the peach-lavender dusk, holds
its hue in the phone's glow, stays pastel, and never competes with the battery's
red. A light overshirt fits a mild early-summer evening (`world.rules.season`: light
clothes, no coats). The soft fade says it is his favourite and much worn, like the
looked-after car: kind wear, never grit. *Rejected:* a zipped hoodie (hood breaks the
tuft silhouette), red or pink (fights the battery), a graphic tee as his only
layer (text risk, no silhouette).

**Why the rest (DECISION, `characters.ren.wardrobe.outfit`):** the oat tee is the
pale centre strip. It is warm off-white, not white, so it never becomes a brighter
shape than the phone near his face (ART_DIRECTION_BIBLE principle 2). The darker
trousers put his value mass on the upper body, where the acting is. Pale shoes
mark his feet in the sprint and at the kerb.

**The jacket is isolated on purpose.** The jacket colour is a single canon entry
(`value.color`), separate from the rest of the outfit. A colour change is one
change request, and `fm impact canon:characters.ren.wardrobe.jacket` lists exactly
the shots and documents it touches. Nothing else in the design depends on the
specific hue, only on these three constraints:
- it is not red or pink;
- it is a mid value, darker than the oat tee;
- it separates from the golden-hour and dusk palette.

A replacement colour should keep all three, or say why not in its rationale.

### Movement (`characters.ren.movement`)
| Phase | Tempo | Body |
|---|---|---|
| Before the turn (SC01–SC03) | Quick, jerky bursts between held poses | Phone close to his face in both hands, elbows tucked, neck forward. Setbacks run **freeze → sag → reset**. Runs with the phone held out in front like a compass, arms not swinging. Drops to his knees in one quick move. |
| The turn (SC04) | Slow, deliberate | No reset. Sits on the kerb, knees up, arms loose over them. The reach for the crank is slow. He holds it in both hands first, then cranks with his right hand and holds the phone in his left, with a steady, even rhythm (about 1 turn/s, RECOMMENDATION). Breath in time with the handle; shoulders rise and fall. Unhurried thumb. |
| After the send (SC04 end, SC06) | Still | Shoulders drop, spine loosens, he leans back against the car, head tips up to the sky. The dark phone rests on his chest under one flat hand. |

- **Handedness:** right-handed. He cranks with the right (the stronger hand
  drives the arm) and types slowly with the left thumb. The screenwriter's
  fallback (crank, pause, type, crank) stays with the animation-director.
- *Rejected:* frantic running with flailing arms (manic slapstick) and a fast,
  strained crank (desperation, not tenderness).

### Arc in body language (`story.arc.ren`, LOCKED; implemented above)
The arc is one change: **from resetting upward to settling down.** In SC01–SC03
every setback ends with him straightening up to try again. At the kerb he
straightens once, to reach for the crank, and then only settles further:
cranking, sending, letting go, leaning back. A viewer who sees only his outline
should be able to tell the two halves of the film apart.

### Continuity rules for Ren (must never change unless the story says so)
| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Crown tuft | Always up. Pressing it down in SC01 is a momentary action. Whether its spring back is seen is the animation-director's call, but in every following shot it is up. |
| Jacket | On, open, sleeves pushed up, in every Ren scene. Never zipped, buttoned, removed or tied round the waist. |
| Oat T-shirt | Always visible as a centre strip between the jacket fronts when he faces camera. |
| Colours | Exactly the canon hexes (after look-director adjustments, the canon values). No per-shot tinting of wardrobe except through lighting. |
| Accessories | None. No watch or bag appears in any shot. |
| Knees and clothes | Clean throughout; no dirt, tears or stains appear. |
| Phone | In his hand or on his chest; never in a pocket on screen after SC01 (RECOMMENDATION, for the continuity owner). |
| Cable | Continuity owner (cinematographer) tracks it. RECOMMENDATION: it stays plugged into the phone and loops in his phone hand while he runs, which gives one continuous object car → shop → kerb. |
| Height | 1.72 m in every set; no cheating scale per shot without a `style_break`. |

## Hana

### Identity (`characters.hana.identity`)
Hana is 20 and Ren's university friend. She has no arc of her own; her one
moment is noticing (`story.arc.hana`). She missed his call innocently: she was
sketching with big headphones on. She does not know how he feels.
- **Defining behaviour (DECISION):** absorbed in making things. Head down over
  paper, pencil moving, lost in it. This answers "is she ignoring him?" with "no,
  she was lost in a drawing". It also rhymes quietly with his cranking: two
  people busy with their hands under the same sky.
- That she studies something involving drawing is an ASSUMPTION and is never
  shown.
- Carries: her phone (on the desk), headphones, sketchbook, pencils.

### Silhouette (`characters.hana.silhouette`)
She sits at her desk under the west window and is seen in left profile (facing
screen left, the window ahead of her face), side-lit by the dusk sky, so her
outline has to tell the story before her face does.
1. **Rounded bob, headphones on top.** A jaw-length rounded bob with a short
   straight fringe makes a smooth head shape, and the big pale headphones sit on
   it. The outline reads "headphones on, can't hear".
2. **Loose, dropped-shoulder top.** A rounded, pear-like upper body, head tilted
   down over the desk.
3. **Two states.** Headphones on the head, then around the neck. The change of
   outline *is* the story beat.

Rounded where Ren is angular, compact where he is tall: the cut from him to her
reads as a new person at thumbnail size.

*Rejected:* long hair (it would tangle with the headphones around her neck, a
collision the proxy cannot solve, and it blurs the headphone outline); a hood or
high collar (no room for the headphones on the neck).

### Proportions (`characters.hana.proportions`)
| Measure | Value | Why / check against the set |
|---|---|---|
| Height | 1.60 m | Clearly smaller than Ren; fits a room built for ~1.7 m adults |
| Build | slight, soft | |
| Heads tall | 6.3 (head 0.254 m) | Same stylisation as Ren (head, eyes, hands ~1.1x) |
| Shoulders / hips | 0.36 / 0.33 m | |
| Hands | 0.185 m, 1.1x | Pencil, headphones, phone |
| Seated | chair seat 0.44 (ASSUMPTION), desk 0.72: eye 1.14, crown 1.27, with headphones 1.30 | |
| Against the window | sill 0.9, head top 1.30: **her head sits inside the window band** | In her left profile (SC05) the dusk sky, the same sky as his, is in frame ahead of her face |

The window placement is the one visual link between two people who never share a
frame (`world.rules.time_and_sun`). Her proportions keep her head level with the
window band, so her profile and the sky share the frame.

### Face and hair (`characters.hana.face`)
- **Face:** round and soft, small chin.
- **Brows:** gently arched.
- **Eyes:** large and rounded, with **visible lower lids**. Her smile lives in
  the lids lifting as much as in the mouth, which a toon face carries more
  reliably than subtle mouth shapes.
- **Nose:** tiny. **Mouth:** small.
- **Hair:** jaw-length rounded bob, near-black warm brown `#2F2629`; short
  straight fringe cut **above the brows**, so her eyes are never covered, even
  with the headphones on.
- **Colours (proposed):** skin `#F1D3BE`, eyes `#4A3A33`, brows `#2A2224`.
- **No blush** (DECISION). A blush would tell the audience she has feelings for
  him and close the open ending (`tone.ending_restraint`; she does not know how
  he feels). It also keeps pink off her face.
- No make-up detail, no accessories.

### Expressions (`characters.hana.expressions`)
Four states in four seconds (SC05), about one per second:

| State | Face |
|---|---|
| Absorbed | eyes down on the page, brows relaxed, mouth softly closed |
| Noticing | eyes flick to the lit phone, brows lift a touch |
| Reading | eyes down to the screen, face lit from below and the side by the phone |
| **Small smile** | lower lids lift, mouth corners rise with lips closed, head dips slightly; no teeth |

**Excluded:** grin, gasp, blush, hand to mouth, squeal. Each would answer more
than the invitation asked. The small smile reads as "yes to dinner", pleased and
a little shy, without "I love you too" (`tone.ending_restraint`).

**Staging risk and fallback (for the cinematographer, flagged per the
character-design skill):** the smile is the film's only answer, and it rests on
a proxy toon face. If the G6 face test shows the lids and mouth corners do not
read, recommended staging, in order:
1. A tighter close-up held longer (borrow 1–2 s from the crank, as the
   screenwriter noted).
2. Let the phone's light arrive on her face as the smile lands, so the light
   change carries it.
3. Show the head dip and the settle of her shoulders in a medium profile shot
   side-lit by the window, where the gesture reads without facial detail.

| Accessories | — | — | None: no jewellery, no watch |

**Why (DECISION):**
- The **crew neck** leaves a clean collar line for the headphones to rest on.
- The loose knit says she is comfortable at home, not dressed to go out: she has
  no idea an invitation is coming.
- **Pale sage** separates her from the warm lamp and from the peach-lavender sky
  in the window, and it is a different hue from Ren's blue, so the cut reads as a
  new person and a new place.
- **Cream headphones** are light-valued, so they stand out against her
  near-black hair in every light, including backlight. That is the one place
  they have to read.

*Rejected:* going-out clothes (as if she expects him), pink or red (the battery's
colour, and pink reads as romantic signalling), dark headphones (they vanish
against her hair).

### Movement (`characters.hana.movement`)
- **Idle:** head tilted down, only the right hand moves in small pencil strokes,
  with a slow breath cycle.
- **Noticing order:** eyes first, then head, then hands. This is a clear staging
  order that makes four actions readable in 4 s.
- **Headphones:** one smooth two-handed slide down to the neck. Gentle and
  curious, not startled.
- **Phone:** picked up with one hand and held low near her chest, so her face
  stays clear for the smile and the phone lights her from below as a new light.
- **Smile:** a small settle of the shoulders. She does not stand, clutch the phone
  or make any big gesture.

### Arc in body language (`story.arc.hana`, LOCKED)
None of her own, by design. Her body goes from **closed** (headphones on, head
down, turned into her work) to **open** (headphones off, head up, turned toward
the phone). It is the smallest possible opening, and it mirrors his settling:
he lets go, she opens up.

## Representation (M3 and later)
`characters.ren.representation` and `characters.hana.representation`:
`provider: proxy` now.

- **One system for both characters (DECISION):** the same humanoid skeleton
  with standard bone names and the same drawn toon-face system (brows, eyes,
  mouth as swappable shapes or textures). This keeps them in one style and
  halves the rig work.
- **Detail budget:** Ren gets a separate open-jacket shell and a tuft mesh.
  Hana gets a rounded bob mesh, a loose-top shell and headphone attach points
  (head and neck). Her body below the desk is simple.
- **Face controls** are listed in canon, per character, and match the expression
  tables above exactly. Nothing more is needed. No lip-sync.
- **Attach points:**
  - Ren: phone in the left hand, crank charger and key in the right hand, phone
    on his chest in SC06.
  - Hana: headphones at the head and neck positions; pencil and phone in the
    right hand.
- **Provider-agnostic:** the entries describe features, measurements, controls
  and colours, not a mesh file. MPFB, VRM, a generated mesh or mocap can replace
  the proxy through one change request to `.representation`, and it must
  reproduce the same height, head count, silhouette features, wardrobe and
  colours. Shots depend on the canon ids, not on a model.
- **Assets (FACT, ORIGINALITY):** no external base mesh is assumed. If one is
  used later, its licence is UNKNOWN until verified.
