# Storyboard: Last Signal

PROPOSED for G5. Text frames (M2); image frames come later. For every shot:
**Frame** (layers front to back, where the subject sits), **Eye** (where the
viewer looks first, then next), **Moves** (what moves; the camera is static
unless stated), **Sound** (cue only; the sound design is not written yet) and
**Out** (transition). Timings are frames at 24 fps; camera values, rationale and
continuity refs are in `08_shots/<id>.shot.yaml`; the table is SHOT_LIST.md.

Wordless throughout (`tone.wordless`): no voices. The only readable text is the
invitation (Option A, see SHOT_LIST.md). Screen left is west in every scene.

## SQ01 · The comedy (golden hour)

### SC01 · INT. REN'S CAR (528 f)

**SC01_SH010 · 40 f · MCU · 50 mm frontal through the windscreen · eye level**
- Frame: FG the rear-view mirror top left, pale glass highlights on the frame
  edges; MG Ren centred, sun full on his face, phone low in his left hand; BG the
  dim back seats. Frame top under the headliner.
- Eye: his face, then his hand and the tuft.
- Moves: he tilts the mirror to himself, presses the tuft down, tilts it back.
- Sound: quiet street, a bird, the car ticking in the heat.
- Out: FADE IN at the head; cut.

**SC01_SH020 · 38 f · insert · 85 mm · screen (0.67 m, above his left shoulder)**
- Frame: the whole phone screen square to us: pale unlabelled blocks and roads,

  the screen's edge; lap soft behind. His head is out of the sight line.
- Eye: the pin, straight away; it holds 38 f (minimum 36).
- Moves: nothing but a small settle of the thumb.
- Sound: a soft UI tap.
- Out: cut.

**SC01_SH030 · 28 f · insert · 85 mm · upper half of the screen (0.30 m, his eyeline)**

**SC01_SH040 · 30 f · MCU · 24 mm from the passenger seat · eye level**

**SC01_SH060 · 34 f · MS · 24 mm, passenger door wild · mild low**

## Checks carried by this storyboard
- Two gags, not one repeated (`camera.gags.no_repeat`): the car is told in
  inserts in the order insert, reaction, insert, reaction (SC01_SH120-SH150); the
  shop in the room: over-shoulder hope, a wary look where nothing happens, the
  exhale, the room-wide blackout, then the insert, then the longest held
  reaction (SC03_SH020-SH070).
- The 12 m sprint is three elliptical cuts of 18, 14 and 16 frames (SC02).
- The 1 % stutter comes before the look to the glovebox (SC04_SH020).
- The jacket never meets the upper dusk sky: every SC04-SC06 lens is at or above
  his jacket top except SC04_SH040, which looks north with the car filling
  everything behind him (he kneels, never stands).
- Nothing moves after the send; the only camera moves in the film are the two
  SC04 push-ins.
- No sky-only, wire-featured or sun-disc frames; no dissolves; the kerb's sky is
  seen only in the bookend, and her window is its pair, never cut against it.
