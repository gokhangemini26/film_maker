# World Bible — Last Signal

Everything here is PROPOSED for G3. Canon ids in brackets are the commitments;
this document explains them. Locked intent, tone and story canon are constraints
and are not re-decided here. Colour, light *style* and characters belong to the
look-director and character-designer; this bible states only what exists, where,
when and why.

## Overview
**DECISION** (`world.era`, `world.setting`). Present day, in an unnamed,
fictional small-town neighbourhood with no country markers. Its vocabulary comes
from the everyday townscapes of Japanese animation: low two-storey houses behind
low garden walls, utility poles strung with overhead wires, pole-mounted street
lamps, a small corner shop with a striped awning. These pieces are combined into
an original place, not a copy of any real street or any known film's location.
Traffic drives on the left, so Ren's right-hand-drive car has its passenger door
and glovebox on the pavement side.

What the world says: this is an ordinary, kindly place on an ordinary warm
evening. Everyone else is indoors having dinner. Nothing is against him except
timing.

## Rules

### Era and technology — `world.era`
- **DECISION.** Contemporary. Touchscreen smartphones charge over USB; Ren's old
  car has a physical key and a 12 V dash socket; newer wall sockets have USB ports
  built in. Every object in the plot is recognisable on sight, which a wordless
  race needs.

### No readable text anywhere — `world.rules.no_readable_text`
- **DECISION** (implements the LOCKED `tone.wordless`). No surface in the world
  carries letters or words: no shop name, signs, posters, packaging text, price
  tags, dashboard legends, car badges, number-plate characters, house numbers,
  street names, book spines or sketchbook writing.
- Information is carried by **pictograms, simple shapes and colour blocks**:
  the shop sign is a basket pictogram; packets are coded by shape and colour
  family; posters are pastel shapes (a drawn ice lolly, a starburst).
- Numbers are allowed by canon but kept to the phone UI. **No clocks** anywhere
  (a clock would be a second meter; `story.escalation` has one meter). **Blank
  number plates**; the cinematographer keeps plates out of feature framing.
- The only text in the film is the typed invitation on Ren's phone (and the same
  text, if Hana's screen is shown).
- No fake or blurred "text-like" scribble either: it still reads as text and can
  be mistaken for a real script.

### Weather — `world.rules.weather`
- **DECISION.** Clear, dry and calm for the whole film. This resolves the
  untagged `weather: clear` in SCENES.yaml (G2 review, finding 12).
- A few **thin high clouds** sit low in the western sky and catch the sunset and
  afterglow; they give the pastel sky depth without simulation.
- **Calm air**: no wind in hair, clothes, awning or wires. Held anime poses stay
  still, and nothing costs animation time.
- Rejected: rain (gritty, melancholy, and a reason to stay in the car); a
  cloudless sky (a flat gradient reads as a backdrop).

### Season — `world.rules.season`
- **DECISION.** Early summer, a mild, warm evening. The sun sets a little after
  seven, so "dinner at 8" follows the dusk naturally and sitting on a warm kerb is
  comfortable, never miserable. Light clothing (the character-designer's call).

### Time and the sun — `world.rules.time_and_sun`
- **DECISION.** The street runs **east–west**; the low sun sits at the **west
  end, along the street's axis**. Ren's car faces west, so the sun pours straight
  through the windscreen (SC01).
- **SC01–SC03** are one continuous golden-hour stretch of a few minutes.
- **The sunset happens off screen**, in the cut between SC03 and SC04 (about ten
  minutes pass while he sits in the dark shop and walks back; this also covers
  the 2% → 1% step, G2 review finding 9).
- **SC04–SC06** are one continuous stretch of civil twilight.
- **Dusk consistency:** SC05 ("evening" in SCENES) is the *same minute* as the
  send. Her west-facing window shows the same afterglow and the same kind of
  roofline, poles and wires in silhouette as his street. Her window and his sky
  must be matched as one sky (DEPENDENCY for the look-director, and for
  continuity at G5). Shots for SC05 should carry both words, e.g.
  `time_of_day: evening (dusk)`, so the deterministic continuity check against
  SCENES.yaml still matches (RECOMMENDATION).
- **No moon in frame.** Whether one early star shows in the SC06 sky is left to
  the look-director (RECOMMENDATION: at most one, faint, or none; anything more
  drifts toward saccharine, `tone.anti_goals`).

### Power — `world.rules.power`
- **DECISION.** The street and the rest of the neighbourhood have power the whole
  time. Only the shop fails (`story.locations`, `blackout_scope: shop_only`).
- **The car:** the old engine idles rough and stalls; its weak old battery goes
  flat with it, so the dash, the socket and the adapter's ring light all go out
  together. It does not restart. No smoke, steam or particles.
- **The shop:** it runs on one old circuit. The trip is caused by the fridge
  compressors cutting in at the moment Ren's plug goes in: the world's timing,
  not his fault (`tone.comedy_source` excludes humiliation). The audience only
  sees and hears plug → bolt → a *clunk* → fridges stop → dark. The shop has **no
  emergency lighting** and **stays dark for the rest of the film**.
- **The street lamps** are photocell lamps that come on **off screen**, before
  SC04. Lamps never switch on or off on screen, and the shop never comes back
  on: after the turn, either event would be an ironic gag (`tone.the_turn`).
- **No other power source is in reach:** no vending machines, public charging
  points, other open shops, power banks, passers-by. This closes every "why
  didn't he just…?", so only his own hands are left (`intent.earned_last_signal`).

### Nobody else — `world.rules.nobody_else`
- **DECISION.** No one but Ren appears on the street or in the shop. The street
  is a **dead end** stopping at a low railing above a slope, so there is no
  through traffic. At dinner time, households are indoors: **lit, curtained
  windows, never figures**.
- The shopkeeper is **implied, never seen**: a doorway with a hanging strip
  curtain behind the counter, into a back room. This is allowed by `story.cast`
  and saves a proxy character, and nobody is there to ask for help.

### One cable, one port — `world.rules.one_port`
- **DECISION.** Every power source offers the same USB port shape: an adapter in
  the car's dash socket (SC01), a wall socket with built-in USB ports in the shop
  (SC03), and the crank charger (SC04). Ren uses **one cable** for all three.
- The three plug-ins become a visual rhyme: the same small gesture fails twice
  and works the third time.
- The cable goes car → shop → kerb; continuity tracks it (G2 finding 12).

## Locations

### 1. The street — `world.locations.street`, `world.sets.street`
**Scenes:** SC01 (through the windscreen), SC02, SC04, SC06; background of SC03
through the door glass.

- **Description.** A short, quiet residential street, about 60 m long, running
  east–west. Low two-storey houses sit behind 1 m garden walls on both sides,
  with raised pavements. Utility poles on the south side carry overhead wires;
  two of them carry street lamps. Ren's car is parked at the **south kerb facing
  west**. The **corner shop** is on the same side, two doors west, about **12 m**
  from the car. The **west end** stops at a low railing above a slope, so the
  western sky is open to the horizon over distant low rooftops. The east end
  fades into more of the same houses.
- **Scale (metres).** Carriageway 5.5 · pavements 1.8 · kerb 0.12 · houses
  6.0–7.5 high · garden walls 1.0 · utility poles 9.0 · lamps at 5.0.
  The full coordinate frame is in `world.sets.street` and the Art Direction
  Bible.
- **Lamps.** One lamp pole just west of the shop (15.5 m from the car), one
  18 m east. **The car sits in the unlit gap between them**: at dusk his kerb
  gets only sky light and the phone, while the lamp by the shop lights the shop's
  dark front in the background.
- **Materials and age.** Dry, fine-grained asphalt, pale with age; concrete
  kerbs with softly worn edges; plastered and panelled house walls; painted
  metal poles; dark wire lines. The neighbourhood is about thirty years old, kept
  tidy: no litter, no graffiti, no broken glass.
- **Sound of the place.** Very quiet. Golden hour: a distant bird, a faraway
  hum of traffic from beyond the slope, his running feet, the shop's door bell.
  Dusk: almost nothing, which is what lets the crank's ratchet and his breath be
  the whole soundtrack (`tone.the_turn`). No sirens, no music from windows, no
  dogs barking (each would be a gag or a mood the film does not want). Sound
  design belongs to the audio department; this is what the world contains.
- **Storytelling details** (each tied to an intent):
  1. **Lit windows at dusk, no figures:** other households sitting down to the
     dinner he is trying to arrange. Warm, not lonely (`intent.open_hopeful_ending`).
  2. **The shop's dark front under a working street lamp** in the background of
     SC04/SC06: the failure stays on record, quietly, without a gag
     (`intent.comic_then_tender`, `intent.race_against_battery`).
  3. **Wires crossing the open western sky:** the film's anime signature, and the
     same lines Hana sees from her window (`intent.anime_feel`,
     `intent.soft_but_cinematic`).
  4. **The open passenger door over the pavement** in SC04: a frame within the
     frame for the turn, and the doorway to the glovebox
     (`intent.earned_last_signal`).

### 2. Ren's car — `world.locations.ren_car`, `world.sets.ren_car`
**Scenes:** SC01 (interior), SC02 (he leaves it), SC04 and SC06 (he sits at its
kerb side).

- **Description.** A small, old two-door hatchback, right-hand drive, 15–20
  years old: his first car, bought cheap and looked after. Sun-faded paint, a
  small dent low on one rear corner, a slightly shiny, worn driver's seat. Rounded
  body and round headlamps: a friendly shape, never a wreck. **No badges, no
  logos, blank plates.** It never moves.
- **Scale.** 3.40 m long × 1.48 m wide × 1.45 m high. Glovebox 0.75 m from the
  driver's hand.
- **Interior (front half only).** A physical key in the ignition; a simple
  dashboard with **unlabelled** round dials (tick marks, no numerals) and a few
  plain buttons; a 12 V socket on the centre console holding a small USB adapter
  with a thin ring light; a rear-view mirror he can tilt; a drop-down glovebox on
  the passenger side. Rear seats and the boot are dark and simple.
- **Glovebox contents:** the crank charger, the charging cable, a few paper
  napkins. Nothing else: no torch, no power bank, nothing that asks "why not use
  that?".
- **Sound.** Key turn; a cough and a rough idle; a sigh, shudder and silence
  when it stalls. The glovebox lid drops with a plastic clack; the charger lands
  with a solid clunk.
- **Storytelling details:**
  1. **Faded paint, cared-for interior:** he doesn't have much, and looks after
     what he has. We are on his side (`tone.register`, `intent.comic_then_tender`).
  2. **The adapter's ring light going out** next to his plug: the car's failure
     told in one small light (`intent.race_against_battery`).
  3. **The glovebox faces the pavement:** the object he rejected sits within
     reach of where he ends up, so the payoff is a look and a reach, not a search
     (`intent.earned_last_signal`).
- **Door continuity (decided by the human).** In SC02 he scrambles across the
  passenger seat and out of the kerb-side passenger door, leaving it open; it
  stands open in SC04 and SC06 (`continuity.props.passenger_door`).

### 3. The corner shop — `world.locations.corner_shop`, `world.sets.corner_shop`
**Scenes:** SC02 (exterior, he runs in), SC03 (interior), SC04/SC06 (dark
exterior in the background).

- **Description.** A small, deep, narrow neighbourhood shop, open late. The
  front **faces north**, so the low western sun never comes in. Its light is its
  own: ceiling panels, three upright drinks fridges glowing on the back wall, a
  lit fascia sign with a **basket pictogram**. A striped awning. The window and
  door glass are mostly covered from inside by shelf backs and pastel pictogram
  posters. A small mechanical bell over the door.
- **Scale.** 4.5 m wide × 9.0 m deep × 2.7 m ceiling. Two low aisles (1.5 m
  gondolas) run from the door to the back. Counter to the left of the door, with
  a plain till box (no screen) and the back-room doorway behind it.
- **The socket.** A plain double wall socket with two USB ports, 0.3 m off the
  floor in the back right-hand corner, behind a freestanding tiered display stand.
  He has to cross the whole shop and kneel to reach it.
- **Materials and age.** Vinyl floor tiles, slightly glossy, scuffed down the
  aisles; powder-coated shelving; glossy fridge glass; semi-gloss packets. About
  twenty years of steady use, clean.
- **Merchandise.** Snacks, drinks, cups of noodles, sweets, household odds and
  ends, told only by **shape and colour family**: round tins, tall boxes,
  pillow bags, bottles with coloured caps. No labels, no prices.
- **Sound.** Door bell; the fridges' steady hum; the ceiling panels' faint buzz.
  At the blackout: a single *clunk* (the breaker, off screen), the hum dies, and
  silence.
- **After the blackout.** Near-dark at the back, where he kneels. The only
  lights are his phone on his face and, far behind him, the pale rectangle of the
  door glass (golden-hour street light reflected in from outside, dim and
  indirect). This is what makes "his lit face is the only light" true to the
  world.
- **Storytelling details:**
  1. **The socket hidden low behind the stand:** effort and a public kneel, so
     this failure costs more than the car (`tone.comedy_source`,
     `intent.race_against_battery`).
  2. **The fridges straight in front of him** go dark and silent: he watches the
     power leave (`intent.comic_then_tender`).
  3. **The strip curtain to an empty back room:** someone runs this place, but
     nobody is there to help (`intent.earned_last_signal`).

### 4. Hana's room — `world.locations.hana_room`, `world.sets.hana_room`
**Scene:** SC05.

- **Description.** A small upstairs bedroom in a similar low-rise neighbourhood
  elsewhere in town (never placed relative to his street). Her desk sits under a
  **west-facing window** with curtains open, showing the same dusk: afterglow,
  a band of rooftops, a utility pole and wires in silhouette. A small arm desk
  lamp is on. On the desk: an open sketchbook with a drawing in progress, pencils
  in a cup, her phone **face-up** to the right of the sketchbook. Four to six
  sketches pinned above the desk. Tidy, lived-in, warm. Upstairs, so the window
  clears the neighbours' roofs and the sky fills the top of the frame.
- **Scale.** 3.0 m × 3.6 m, ceiling 2.4 m. Window 1.2 × 1.1 m, sill at 0.9 m.
  Desk 1.1 × 0.55 m at 0.72 m.
- **Materials.** Matte wood desk, paper, soft fabric curtains, a painted plaster
  wall; bed edge, shelf and door kept soft and simple in shadow.
- **Sound.** Almost silent from outside; the scratch of pencil. What leaks from
  her headphones, and the phone's arrival sound, are the audio department's.
- **What she draws** (`world.locations.hana_room.sketches`): ordinary things
  only (her window view, rooftops and wires, a plant, a cat, hands, cups).
  **Never Ren, never a heart, never writing.** A drawing of him would tell the
  audience she already loves him and close the ending (`tone.ending_restraint`).
- **Her phone is not charging.** A charger cable on her desk would be an ironic
  gag after the turn (`tone.the_turn`).
- **Storytelling details:**
  1. **The same sky in her window** as over his kerb: the only visual link between
     two people who never share a frame (`intent.open_hopeful_ending`,
     `intent.soft_but_cinematic`).
  2. **Big over-ear headphones and a sketch in progress:** she was absorbed, not
     ignoring him, told in two objects (`story.arc.hana`,
     `intent.open_hopeful_ending`).
  3. **A lit, settled, complete room** after his emptied street: the first frame
     that feels whole (STORY_STRUCTURE beat 8, `intent.soft_but_cinematic`).

## What the world never shows
- **Any readable text** except the invitation, including clocks, plates, house
  numbers and pseudo-script (`world.rules.no_readable_text`).
- **Other people.** No passers-by, drivers, figures in windows or shopkeeper
  (`world.rules.nobody_else`).
- **Other power sources** within reach: vending machines, public charging
  points, other open shops, power banks, a torch or solar panel on the charger
  (`world.rules.power`, `world.props.crank_charger`).
- **The sunset itself**, lamps switching on, or the shop's power coming back
  (`world.rules.time_and_sun`, `world.rules.power`).
- **Smoke, steam, sparks or particles** from the car or the shop.
- **Weather events:** rain, wind, fog (`world.rules.weather`).
- **The restaurant, the dinner, a map with street names** (`tone.ending_restraint`,
  `tone.wordless`).
- **A drawing of Ren or a heart** anywhere in Hana's room; a charging cable on
  her desk.
- **Brands, logos, trademarks** and anything modelled on an identifiable real
  street, shop or car.
- **Grit:** litter, graffiti, broken glass, rust holes (`tone.anti_goals`: the
  world stays kind; never melodrama).

## Feasibility notes (M3, Blender 5.2 EEVEE toon, laptop)
- **Build in detail:** the car (the one hero set; exterior plus the front half of
  the interior), the shop's back third (fridges, socket, stand, the end of one
  aisle), Hana's window wall and desk, and the hero props (phone, crank charger,
  cable, headphones).
- **Build as blocks and silhouettes:** houses (extruded boxes with window insets
  and a few emissive window cards at dusk), garden walls, poles (cylinders),
  wires (curves), the shop's front aisles (arrayed boxes), the distant rooftops
  beyond the railing (flat silhouette cards against the sky).
- **Let darkness and distance do the rest:** the shop after the blackout, the
  back of the car interior, the rest of Hana's room, the east end of the street.
- **Sky:** a painted or gradient sky with a few cloud cards, not a simulation
  (the creative direction asks for gradient skies over volumetrics).
- **Flagged risks (for the cinematographer and animation-director):**
  - **The cable** is the hardest object: a free-hanging cable that moves with his
    hands. Recommend static bezier shapes swapped per shot, or framing that keeps
    most of its length out of frame. No cloth or rope simulation in the MVP.
  - **Crank handle** rotation is one hinge plus one spin axis: simple to rig.
  - **Headphones sliding down** are a rigid prop with two poses (on head, round
    neck): buildable, as the G2 review notes.
  - **Car door and glovebox lid** are single hinges.
  - Nothing in the script is impossible at MVP fidelity. No staging alternative
    is needed.
- **Asset licences:** no third-party asset is planned. If any is used (a car
  base mesh, a sky texture), its licence is **UNKNOWN until verified**.
