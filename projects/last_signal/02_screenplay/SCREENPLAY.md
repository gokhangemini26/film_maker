[[Wordless film. No dialogue anywhere. The phone shows only icons, numbers and colour. The typed English invitation is the only readable text. No on-screen title card or credits appear in these scenes; that question (G1 review item 1) is still open and is not the screenplay's to decide.]]

[[The invitation text below uses Option A. Options B and C are in the notes at the end; whichever the human picks at G2 replaces both fragments.]]

FADE IN:

INT. REN'S CAR - GOLDEN HOUR [[SC01]]

[[Beats 1-3. 0-22 s. Setup 0-12 s (call 8 s, text 4 s), car 12-22 s.]]

Low sun pours through the windscreen of a small, old car parked at the kerb. REN (about 20) sits behind the wheel, phone in hand.

He tilts the rear-view mirror toward himself, presses down one stubborn tuft of hair, tilts the mirror back.

On his phone: a map with a single pin, a fork-and-knife icon. No names, no streets.

He swipes. Her photo fills the screen. No name. A green call icon. The battery reads 5%.

He breathes in, the practised kind of breath, and taps call.

The call icon pulses. Rings. Rings. Rings.

No answer. The icon stops.

He lowers the phone to his lap. His shoulders drop a little.

Then he sits up and opens a message. His thumb types:

> Are you free tonight? <

[[Readable text on screen: "Are you free tonight?" (Option A, first fragment). Keyboard letters must not read: see notes.]]

The battery icon turns red. 4%.

He freezes, thumb in the air. A small, stuck exhale.

He turns the key. The old engine coughs, catches, idles rough.

He pops the glovebox for his cable. A chunky HAND-CRANK CHARGER tumbles out and clunks onto his lap: a plastic brick with a fold-out handle.

He looks at it. Not now. He shoves it back into the glovebox, fishes out the cable, plugs in.

A charging bolt appears beside 4%. He sinks back into the seat. Relief.

The engine sighs, shudders and dies. The dash lights go out. The bolt vanishes. 3%.

Slowly, his forehead lowers onto the steering wheel.

EXT. STREET - GOLDEN HOUR [[SC02]]

[[Beat 4, first part. 22-24 s.]]

The car door swings open and Ren is out, running up the empty pavement with the phone held out in front of him, toward a small corner shop a few doors along. A door chime as he ducks inside.

INT. CORNER SHOP - GOLDEN HOUR [[SC03]]

[[Beat 4, second part. 24-34 s.]]

A bright little shop. Fridges hum. Shelves of pastel packets, no readable labels.

Ren scans the walls. There: a socket, low down, behind a display stand. He drops to his knees, reaches behind it, plugs in.

The charging bolt appears beside 3%. He lets out a long breath.

Clunk. The fridges stop humming. Every light in the shop goes out at once.

Silence. In the dark room the only light is the phone, glowing on his face. The bolt is gone. 2%.

He doesn't move.

EXT. STREET, KERB BY REN'S CAR - DUSK [[SC04]]

[[Beats 5-7. 34-53 s. The turn at 34 s. From here on, no gags: quiet and slow (tone.the_turn).]]

The sun has gone. The sky softens toward dusk. Ren sits on the kerb beside his dead car, knees up, spent.

The battery reads 1%, blinking red.

He looks up at the car. The passenger door stands open. The glovebox.

A moment. He leans in, opens it, and takes out the crank charger. This time he holds it in both hands, carefully.

He plugs the phone into it, unfolds the handle, and turns it. Slow. Steady.

Only the ratchet of the handle and his breathing. The street is quiet.

The glow on his face stops flickering and holds.

With his thumb he finishes the message:

> Are you free tonight? Dinner at 8? My treat. <

[[Readable text on screen: the full invitation (Option A).]]

He adds a heart at the end. Looks at it. His thumb hovers.

He deletes the heart.

1% becomes 2%.

His thumb rests over the send arrow. He presses it.

A thin progress line fills. A tick appears. No word.

He lets go of the handle. It clicks to a stop. He breathes out.

INT. HANA'S ROOM - EVENING [[SC05]]

[[Beat 8. 53-57 s.]]

A desk by a window, a lamp on. HANA (about 20) sketches, big headphones over her ears. Her phone lies face-up beside the sketchbook.

It lights up. His photo, no name.

She slides the headphones down around her neck, picks up the phone and reads.

A small smile.

EXT. STREET, KERB BY REN'S CAR - DUSK - MOMENTS LATER [[SC06]]

[[Beat 9. 57-60 s. Final image.]]

Ren's screen dims and fades to black.

He doesn't reach for it. He leans back against the car, the dark phone resting on his chest, and looks up at the dusk sky.

FADE OUT.

SCREENWRITER'S NOTES (not part of the film)

1. INVITATION WORDING - for the human to choose at G2 (story.message)
   Each option is split in two: the first fragment is typed in SC01 (before the
   battery turns red), the rest in SC04 during the crank. All are 12 words or
   fewer, contain no name and confess nothing.

   Option A (plain and confident, 9 words) - used in the draft above
     SC01: "Are you free tonight?"
     SC04: "Are you free tonight? Dinner at 8? My treat."

   Option B (points back to the map pin, 10 words)
     SC01: "Dinner tonight?"
     SC04: "Dinner tonight? There's a place I want to show you."

   Option C (nervous, a little more of a date, 9 words)
     SC01: "Hey, are you free tonight?"
     SC04: "Hey, are you free tonight? Dinner, just us? 8pm?"

   RECOMMENDATION: A. It reads fastest at the send (short sentences, one number),
   and "My treat" makes it clearly an invitation without hinting at the
   confession. B ties the message to the fork-and-knife pin but is the longest
   to read. C is the warmest but "just us" leans toward saying what the heart
   emoji is meant to hold back.

2. TIMING (sums to 60 s; matches STORY_STRUCTURE beat for beat)
