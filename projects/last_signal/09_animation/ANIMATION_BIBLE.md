# Animation Bible — Last Signal

This is the short rulebook behind the 38 `09_animation/*.anim.yaml` files. The vocabulary in
`canon/animation.yaml` is the source of truth. This document explains it and does not add names to it.
Status: PROPOSED. Nothing here is ratified until the human decides G7.

## 1. Movement philosophy

- **DECISION: the movement is the arc.** Ren's change plays out in posture, tempo and stillness
  (`characters.ren.movement`). Before the turn he moves in quick bursts between held poses. After the turn
  he settles and stops resetting. Rationale: `story.arc.ren` is told without words (`tone.wordless`), so
  the body has to carry the turn. Rejected: carrying the change with faces alone, which the proxy face and
  the many off-face shots cannot support. Serves `intent.comic_then_tender`.
- **DECISION: held poses, not rubbery takes.** Every comic reaction is a pose held long enough to read
  (`characters.ren.expressions` comic_style held_pose_reactions). Rationale: this is the anime timing the
  film borrows (`intent.anime_feel`), and the proxy rig reads clean silhouettes better than squash.
  Rejected: overlapping squash-and-stretch takes, which read as Western slapstick and turn manic
  (`tone.anti_goals`).
- **DECISION: only motion a stylised proxy can show.** Cable, hair, jacket and door swing are posed or
  driven by constants, never simulated. Rationale: the MVP has no simulation, and posed props chain across
  cuts. Simulation is an M3+ risk and is not planned (FACT: no anim file asks for one).

## 3. Per-character movement language

### Ren
- **Before the turn (SC01–SC03): bursts and holds.** Pose changes are short blends (FACT: 2–7 f, mostly
  `ease_out` / `ease_in`) that land on a `hold`. The exceptions are the slow sinks, which are meant to
  feel heavy: the relief recline (SC01_SH130, 11 f) and the forehead to the wheel (SC01_SH150, 18 f). Setbacks run freeze → sag → reset (`freeze` face, a sag pose, then
  `determined` on the reset). The phone stays close to the face in both hands, and the run keeps the phone
  arm out and the arms still (`run_phone_out`). DECISION, rationale: the comedy is bad timing plus
  persistence (`tone.comedy_source`). The reset is what makes the next setback funny.
- **The turn (SC04): no reset.** The body settles on `kerb_hunch`, then reaches slowly and deliberately
  (FACT: SC04_SH040 blends are 8–10 f; the one 3 f blend is the crank clamp settling in SC04_SH050),
  then `kerb_crank_hold` + `crank_turn`. The breath is
  `crank_locked`: in on the up half-turn, out on the down. FACT: from SC04_SH030 on, no comic face is
  legal (`comic_illegal_from_shot`, checked by `fm check anim`). DECISION, rationale: the change of key
  has to be visible in the face and the tempo at the same time (`tone.the_turn`).
- **After the send (SC04 end, SC06): release.** `out_long`, `kerb_lean_back`, `final_look_up` with
  `lids: low`, and the dark phone on his chest under a flat hand. Rationale: `tone.ending_restraint`; the
  look up stays short of a smile.
- **Crank tempo.** DECISION: 24 f per handle turn (1 turn/s at 24 fps), one phase source (`handle_top`
  events every 24 f) carried across cuts. Rationale: `characters.ren.movement` RECOMMENDATION of about
  1 turn/s, and the ratchet is the score of the tender half (`audio.ratchet`). Rejected: a faster crank,
  which reads as strain rather than tenderness.

### Hana
- **DECISION: still and economical, in the order eyes → head → hands** (`characters.hana.movement`
  notice_order). `desk_sketch` → `desk_notice` (eyes lead via the look track) → `desk_headphones_off`
  → `desk_phone_low`. The pencil and headphones live on prop tracks so the hands never move before the head.
  The smile (`small_smile`) comes with a slight shoulder settle and a soft blink on `lids`. Rationale: four
  actions have to read in 4 s, and stillness is what makes her room feel complete. Rejected: standing or
  clutching the phone (too big for 4 s; `stands: false`).

## 4. Timing, ease and holds

- **Every hold is explicit** (`ease: hold`), including the last key of every track. A shot never ends on
  an interpolation. FACT: 58 of the 89 pose keys are holds. Rationale: in this film, held frames are the
  acting. An implicit hold lets a builder drift.
- **Eases carry intent, not raw curves.** `ease_out` = a burst that settles (comic). `ease_in_out` =
  deliberate (tender, the default after the turn). `ease_in` = falling with weight (sags, blink closes).
  `gravity` = a fall with one short bounce (the lid and crank drop in SC01_SH100, the knee drop in
  SC03_SH010). `overshoot_small` is used on one pose key (the reset burst, SC01_SH060 f20) and never after
  the turn. Rationale: an overshoot after the turn would put the gag back in.
- **Blinks** (lids track): close 2–4 f, shut 1–2 f, open 3–6 f, at least 6 f in total. Tempos differ on
  purpose: the hinge slow blink in SC03_SH070 is 12 f, the tired and hesitation blinks are 6 f.
- **Hold scopes:** `body`, `face`, `phone` (insert legibility, with `min_f` where the shot sets a minimum
  read time) and `all` (nothing moves, which also blocks lids). FACT: 49 holds across the film
  (17 body, 14 phone, 14 all, 4 face).
- **Speeds.** Walk/run distance ÷ speed is checked per shot in the anim file. The SC03_SH010 aisle sprint
  peaks at 5.0 m/s (stride 1.25 m, a step every 6 f). ASSUMPTION: with the phone arm rigid and the arms
  not swinging, this reads as urgent rather than manic. QA could not judge it without a playblast (see 7.4).

## 5. Event naming

- **DECISION: event ids are `snake_case`, describe what happens rather than how it is cued, are unique
  per shot, and stay stable once the sound-designer cues them.** Examples: `lid_latch`, `plug_in`,
  `heart_off`, `tick_on`. Sequences get a numeric suffix (`flicker_dip_1..3`, `ring_1..3`). Rationale:
  AUDIO_CUES anchors 113 of 119 cues by id (G7 review #15). Renaming an id silently breaks a cue.
- **Kinds:** `sound` (audible, cued by the sound-designer), `light` (blackout, indicators, flicker),
  `state` (a prop or screen change worth naming), `visual` (no sound, e.g. a blink or a contact).
  FACT: 182 events in total: 75 visual, 59 sound, 29 state, 19 light.
- **Generated events:** `handle_top` comes from the `crank_turn` gait every 24 f and is never written by
  hand, so the ratchet has one phase source.
- RECOMMENDATION (optional, G7 review #15): if the human keeps the SC01_SH020 tap (D4 extra), add a
  `sound` event for it so its cue stops being frame-anchored.

## 6. How the anim files serve the intents

|---|---|---|

| `intent.soft_but_cinematic` | no graphic comic symbols, no rubber; slow pushes left to the camera | throughout |

## 7. Known open items

### 7.1 `wary` / `determined` against locked `characters.ren.expressions` (pending human ruling)
FACT: `animation.vocab.v2.face.ren` adds `wary` (SC03_SH020 f0, SC03_SH030 f0) and `determined`
(SC01_SH060 f18, SC02_SH010 f0) to the comic set. The LOCKED `characters.ren.expressions` lists five comic
faces, and no change request has been filed (G7 review #3). DEPENDENCY: either the human rules that
animation faces may refine the listed sets, or the character-designer files a change request. If both are
refused, the fallback is to revert those four keys to `neutral`. That fallback is legal, with no other
file changes. RECOMMENDATION: before G7 locks the entry, add the rejected alternative to its rationale
("`neutral`: loses the expectation half of the wary gag and the reset of freeze-sag-reset"), as G7 review
#19 asks.

### 7.2 Cable hidden in SC04_SH010–SH040 (ASSUMPTION, against locked `continuity.props.cable`)
FACT: the four files key `charging_cable {f: 0, loc: loose, state: hidden}`. The locked canon says the
cable is "always plugged into the phone" and that in wides "most of its length" is out of frame, which
means some of it is visible. `hidden` removes all of it, including the phone end, in shots where the phone
is visible (SH010, SH020 insert, SH040). G7 review #10.

**Proposal (RECOMMENDATION, vocab-legal, not yet applied):** change the key in all four files to
`{f: 0, loc: loose, state: posed}`. FACT: the vocabulary already allows it (`posed` is a legal state and
`loose` a legal loc). FACT: the builder (`blender/fm_blender/animate.py`, `loc == "loose"`) puts the
source end on the ground about 0.12 m beside and 0.14 m in front of his left foot, and the phone end on the
phone. So the cable runs from the phone port down to the pavement by his foot. The chain is unchanged
(`loose` → `hand_r` at SC04_SH050 f0 is a legal transition; only `loc` chains). Expected read per shot
(ASSUMPTION until the playblast):
- SH010 (wide, kerb hunch): a short drop from the phone between his knees to his foot, mostly behind his
  hands and shins. This matches canon.
- SH020 (insert): a plug stub at the phone's bottom edge, where the thumb enters. Check it does not cover
  the keys.
- SH030 (medium close): the phone is low and probably out of frame, so little or nothing shows.
- SH040 (low lunge into the car): the run from the seat edge to the pavement may be 0.6 m or more in view.

**Gap:** the vocabulary has no "stub only" option. It is either the full posed curve or nothing. If the
SH040 run reads as too much cable, the fix is builder routing (along the leg or behind the hand), not a
new name. I did not apply this because the human has not ruled on #10, and editing four anim files makes
the qa:motion and audio/post chain stale again. It is one key per file and can be applied straight after
a ruling.

### 7.3 No thumb track for SC04_SH020 / SC04_SH060
FACT: the vocabulary has no hand or thumb names. In both shots the thumb path lives only in `notes`, so
the builder draws no thumb (G7 review #8). The SC04_SH060 hesitation (stop over the heart 12 f, drift to
backspace, hover 14 f, press) is the most tender beat in the film, and right now it is carried by screen
timing alone.

- **Option A (legal today, no new names):** the builder derives a thumb from the existing `key_press` ui
  events: on the key during each press, 3–5 mm above it between presses, entering and leaving at the frame
  bottom. Limit: the drift to backspace would happen just before the f54 press instead of at f34–40, so the

- **Option B (RECOMMENDATION, minimal v3 proposal):** a per-character `thumb` track with three refs,
